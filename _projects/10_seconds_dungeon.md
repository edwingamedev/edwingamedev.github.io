title: "10 Seconds Dungeon"

summary: "A solo arcade survival game built in 48 hours for Liga Game Jam 3, featuring timed waves, weapon switching, bosses and score-based progression."

description: "A fast-paced arcade survival game where you play as a cat trapped in a dungeon. Every 10 seconds a new wave begins, introducing increasingly difficult enemies, while limited ammunition, health pickups and weapon drops force you to keep moving and adapting. Every 10 waves, a stronger boss appears with different behavior, and the run ends when the player dies."

  - Boss encounters every 10 waves with distinct behavior from regular enemies
  - Weapon switching between AK-47 and shotgun with independent finite ammunition
  - Dynamic ammo pickups that reload the currently equipped weapon
  - Periodic spawning of ammunition, health and weapon pickups around the arena
  - Score-based arcade loop with game-over results
  - Entire game completed solo in 48 hours for Liga Game Jam 3, including programming, game design, pixel art, music and sound effects

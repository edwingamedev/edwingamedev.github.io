title: "End of Your Life"

summary: "An arcade survival game originally developed for mobile during Global Game Jam and now available on the web. Designed and programmed solo, featuring a soul-possession mechanic, deadly dungeon traps and score-based progression."

description: "An arcade survival game where players have only a few seconds left to live. To survive, they must launch their soul out of their current body, navigate through hazards and possess another character before time runs out. Explore a dangerous dungeon, collect skulls, avoid deadly traps and keep possessing new bodies to achieve the highest score."

project_type: "Game Jam"
role: "Solo Game Developer & Pixel Artist"

focus:
  - Gameplay
  - Pixel Art
  - Game Systems

technical_highlights:
  - Designed and developed the game solo for Global Game Jam, originally targeting mobile devices
  - Implemented a soul-possession mechanic where players launch their soul as a controllable projectile to take over other characters
  - Developed distinct movement behavior for characters and souls, including restricted lateral movement while controlling the soul
  - Created three playable character variants with unique sprites and corresponding soul appearances
  - Implemented dungeon hazards including floor spikes, wall-mounted arrow traps and electrical discharges between Tesla coils
  - Developed a collectible currency system using skulls found throughout the dungeon
  - Implemented a revive system allowing players to spend collected currency to continue their run after dying
  - Built a game-over and high-score system tracking the number of bodies possessed and collected currency
  - Created the game's pixel art and integrated audio assets provided by an external collaborator

title: "Space Power Defender"

summary: "A solo-developed space shooter created for my first Ludum Dare game jam and expanded through post-jam updates. Responsible for programming, game design, pixel art, music and sound effects."

description: "A wave-based space shooter where players defend four power cores from incoming enemies. Each core grants a different spaceship ability, including an energy shield, side projectiles, increased speed and faster regeneration. Losing a core temporarily weakens the spaceship, while surviving additional waves allows players to recover lost powers. Fight increasingly difficult enemy waves, protect your abilities and survive as long as possible."

project_type: "Game Jam"
role: "Solo Game Developer"

focus:

  - Pixel Art

technical_highlights:
  - Designed and developed the entire game solo for my first Ludum Dare game jam, including programming, pixel art, music and sound effects
  - Implemented a wave-based enemy spawning system with increasingly difficult encounters and varied wave compositions
  - Developed enemy behavior variations including sine-wave movement, spinning movement and different attack ranges and fire rates
  - Created a power-core system where each core grants a distinct spaceship ability and losing a core removes its associated power
  - Implemented power recovery after every three waves, encouraging players to survive while temporarily weakened
  - Developed spaceship movement, mouse-based aiming, shooting and a player-controlled energy shield
  - Added combat feedback through screen shake, damage blinking and animated cursor effects
  - Iterated on combat balance, enemy health, projectile interactions and wave progression through multiple post-jam updates
  - Created all pixel art using Aseprite, composed music in LMMS and produced sound effects with bfxr
